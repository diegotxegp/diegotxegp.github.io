On Wednesday 13 November 2019, as part of the “Science Week” at the Faculty of Sciences of the University of Cantabria, I gave a talk about the work I have done for my PhD since April. I explained how the synchronisation of physical clocks works in real-time systems, the protocols applied, the components used, the configuration of the experiments and their results.
