My research

The problem

Clinical researchers have data and questions, but building predictive models usually takes programming and AI skills they do not have.

The idea

Automate the technical work (AutoML) while keeping the researcher in control, in a no-code tool that respects the structure of clinical protocols, which generic AutoML tools ignore.

The tool

Hamelin is the desktop application at the core of my thesis, within a funded project at the University of Cantabria (2024–2028).

Try it: a tiny AutoML

Choose a dataset, customise the experiment and train a model right here in your browser. The datasets are real public ones from the benchmarks of my research, and the models are really trained, but this is a small teaching demo written for this page, not Hamelin itself.
